## Executive Summary

Google was the **sleeping giant of AI**. With DeepMind pioneering everything and massive infrastructure investments, Google had all the pieces but was constrained by antitrust concerns. **November 2025 changed everything** - constraints lifted, and Google **let rip**.

**Result:** Gemini 3 is SOTA, and it's **everywhere suddenly**.
