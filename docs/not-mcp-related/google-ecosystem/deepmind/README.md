# Google DeepMind - Research Projects

**Last Updated:** 2025-11-25  
**Status:** Active - Multiple breakthrough projects  
**Role:** Google's AI research powerhouse

---

## Overview

**DeepMind** is Google's AI research division, the **pioneer in everything** AI. With antitrust constraints lifted, DeepMind projects are now being rapidly integrated into Google's product ecosystem.

---

## Active Projects

### Genie 3
**World Model for Interactive Environments**

- **Status:** Latest version (2025)
- **Purpose:** Create interactive, controllable virtual worlds
- **Applications:** Gaming, simulation, training
- **Breakthrough:** Unprecedented world generation quality

### Lyria
**Music Generation Model**

- **Status:** Active development
- **Purpose:** High-quality music generation
- **Features:** Multi-instrument, style control, long-form composition
- **Integration:** YouTube, Google services

### Veo 3
**Video Generation Model**

- **Release:** May 2025
- **Breakthrough:** **Synchronized audio generation**
  - Dialogue
  - Sound effects
  - Ambient noise
- **Quality:** Cinema-grade output
- **Integration:** YouTube, Google services

### AlphaFold
**Protein Folding Prediction**

- **Status:** Ongoing research
- **Impact:** Revolutionized biology research
- **Latest:** AlphaFold 3 (2024)
- **Applications:** Drug discovery, disease research

### Gemma 3
**Open-Source Language Models**

- **Release:** March 2025
- **Purpose:** Lightweight Gemini variants
- **Variants:**
  - **PaliGemma** - Vision-language tasks
  - **MedGemma** - Medical consultations
- **License:** Open-source, commercial-friendly

## Research Focus Areas

### 1. Multimodal AI
- **Text, image, audio, video** - Unified understanding
- **Cross-modal generation** - Create across modalities
- **Context integration** - Rich contextual understanding

### 2. Agent Systems
- **Autonomous agents** - Self-directed AI systems
- **Multi-agent coordination** - Teams of AI agents
- **Tool use** - Agents that use external tools

### 3. Scientific Discovery
- **AlphaFold** - Biology
- **Materials science** - New material discovery
- **Climate modeling** - Environmental research

### 4. Creative AI
- **Lyria** - Music
- **Veo** - Video
- **Imagen** - Images
- **Nano Banana Pro** - Advanced image editing

---

## Integration Timeline

### Pre-November 2025
- Research projects mostly separate
- Limited product integration
- Antitrust constraints

### November 2025 Onwards
- **Rapid integration** into Google products
- **Gemini 3** incorporates DeepMind research
- **Antigravity** uses agent research
- **Nano Banana Pro** uses image research
