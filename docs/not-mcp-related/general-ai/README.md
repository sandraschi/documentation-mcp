## Global Overview

Late 2025 marks a pivotal moment in artificial intelligence history. The pace
of progress that seemed unsustainable has, somehow, sustained. The "AI
Unleashed" era has begun, characterized by several convergent developments
that are reshaping the technological landscape.

### Google's Resurgence

After years of watching OpenAI capture public imagination with ChatGPT, Google
has roared back. The November 2025 release of Gemini 3 wasn't just an
incremental improvement—it represented Google "unleashed" from certain
antitrust constraints that had limited how aggressively the company could
integrate AI across its products.

Alongside Gemini 3 came Antigravity IDE, an agent-first development
environment built by ex-Windsurf engineers Google acqui-hired, and Nano
Banana Pro, their image generation offering. The message was clear: Google is
no longer playing defense.

### Model Maturity

The frontier models of late 2025—GPT-5 (released August 2025), Claude 4.5
(October/November 2025), and Gemini 3 (November 2025)—represent a new baseline
for what AI can do. Mathematical reasoning, complex coding, long-form analysis,
multimodal understanding—capabilities that seemed science fiction three years
ago are now table stakes.

The improvements are real but incremental compared to the GPT-3 to GPT-4 leap.
We may be approaching the limits of scaling laws, or we may be in a temporary
plateau before the next breakthrough. Nobody knows for certain.

### The Hardware Wars

Nvidia's GPU monopoly, long considered unassailable, is showing cracks. Google's
TPU v6, optimized for their own models, offers competitive performance for
inference. Huawei's Ascend 950, developed to circumvent US export controls,
powers China's independent AI ecosystem. Qualcomm and Cerebras offer specialized
alternatives for specific use cases.

The monopoly isn't broken—CUDA's software ecosystem remains formidable—but the
market is more competitive than it's been in years.

### Geopolitical Bifurcation

The split between Western and Chinese AI ecosystems is nearly complete. On one
side: OpenAI, Google, Anthropic running on Nvidia and TPU hardware. On the
other: DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, and other Chinese labs running on Huawei Ascend
chips with domestically developed software stacks.

This isn't just technical divergence—it's the infrastructure for two separate
AI futures, with uncertain implications for the decades ahead.

## Strategic Themes

### The Agentic Shift

The transformation from "chatbot" to "agent" is complete for frontier systems.
Tools like Antigravity, Manus, and Aardvark don't just respond to queries—they
formulate goals, create plans, execute multi-step actions, and adjust based on
results. The AI as conversational partner is evolving into AI as autonomous
worker.

### Hardware-Software Lock-in

Ecosystem competition is intensifying. The Western stack faces an internal
battle between Nvidia's CUDA/PyTorch dominance and Google's TPU/JAX alternative.
The Eastern stack—Huawei CANN and MindSpore—is maturing rapidly, driven by
necessity as sanctions limit access to Western hardware.

### Physical AI

The integration of AI into robotics is accelerating. Figure 03 (October 2025)
and Google's Gemini Robotics (March 2025) bring language model reasoning to
physical bodies. These systems don't just process language—they understand
physical affordances, predict outcomes of actions, and plan manipulation
tasks. The "embodiment" problem is being solved.
