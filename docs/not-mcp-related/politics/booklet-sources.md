## Claims and Corrections — AI Environmental Impact

**Hao, Karen. Empire of AI. Penguin Press, 2025.**
Primary source for water consumption claims. National Book Critics Circle Award for Nonfiction 2026. Read with correction statement.

**Hao, Karen. "Empire of AI — Water Footprint Changes." karendhao.com, December 2025.**
Author's own correction statement. Confirms factor-of-1,000 error, explains unit conversion mistake, provides corrected text.

**Masley, Andy. "Empire of AI is Wildly Misleading About AI Water Use." Substack, November 2025.**
Independent analysis that identified the error. Also publishes "The AI Water Issue Is Fake" — overstates the counter-case but contains essential fact-checking.

**Wired / Steven Levy. "You're Thinking About AI and Water All Wrong." December 2025.**
Mainstream coverage of the correction. Acknowledges error while noting real environmental concerns remain.

## Yudkowsky and AI Safety Discourse

**Yudkowsky, Eliezer. LessWrong corpus. lesswrong.com, 2006–present.**
Primary source for p(doom) estimates, AI risk frameworks, basilisk episode. Extensive. Read critically.

**Yudkowsky, Eliezer. "Pausing AI Developments Isn't Enough. We Need to Shut It All Down." Time, March 2023.**
The data center bombing proposal. Direct intellectual ancestry of operational Butlerism.

**RationalWiki. "Roko's Basilisk."**
Most complete documentation of the basilisk episode including Yudkowsky's suppression response and subsequent Streisand effect. rationalwiki.org.

**Russell, Stuart. Human Compatible: Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control. Viking, 2019.**
The serious AI safety alternative to Yudkowsky. Specific, bounded, falsifiable concerns about alignment. Essential counter-reading.

**Homeland Security Today. "When AI Disruption Meets Radicalization: A New Counterterrorism Risk Emerges." April 2026.**
Professional counterterrorism analysis of the M-G incident and broader anti-AI radicalization pipeline. hstoday.us.

## Historical Cases — Violence

**Jensen, Richard Bach. The Battle Against Anarchist Terrorism: An International History, 1878–1934. Cambridge UP, 2014.**
The definitive scholarly treatment of the anarchist bombing campaigns across Europe and America. Not hero worship. Essential.

**Maitron, Jean. Histoire du mouvement anarchiste en France. 2 vols. 1951–1955.**
The foundational French-language scholarship on anarchism. Covers the intellectual-to-operational pipeline in exhaustive detail.

**Kaczynski, Theodore. Industrial Society and Its Future. 1995.**
The Unabomber manifesto. Published under duress by Washington Post and New York Times. Read as radicalization document, not philosophy.

**Chase, Alston. Harvard and the Unabomber: The Education of an American Terrorist. Norton, 2003.**
The best biographical treatment of Kaczynski's radicalization. Covers the Harvard psychology experiments that may have contributed.

## Historical Cases — Non-Violence

**Clarkson, Thomas. The History of the Rise, Progress and Accomplishment of the Abolition of the African Slave-Trade by the British Parliament. 2 vols. 1808.**
Primary source. Available free digitally. The evidence-gathering methodology described in Part Two. Still readable, still instructive.

**Hochschild, Adam. Bury the Chains: Prophets and Rebels in the Fight to Free an Empire's Slaves. Houghton Mifflin, 2005.**
The best modern narrative history of the British abolition movement. Readable, rigorously sourced.

**Purvis, June. Emmeline Pankhurst: A Biography. Routledge, 2002.**
Serious biography of the WSPU leader. Covers the internal debates about the boundary between property destruction and personal violence.

**McPherson, James. Battle Cry of Freedom: The Civil War Era. Oxford UP, 1988.**
The standard one-volume Civil War history. Covers John Brown's role and the Southern response that accelerated secession.

## West Africa Squadron — Critical Sources

**Burroughs, Robert and Huzzey, Richard (eds.). Reconsidering the West Africa Squadron: Suppressed Narratives and New Approaches to British Anti-Slavery. Liverpool UP, 2016.**
The essential critical volume. Emma Christopher's chapter on liberated Africans in Sierra Leone is the key text for the "freedom's fine print" argument.

**Lester, Alan. "The West Africa Squadron Memorial: An Exercise in Virtue Signalling and Denial." alanlester.co.uk, 2024.**
Polemical but analytically useful. Argues the Squadron narrative functions as self-congratulatory historical cover.

**International Journal of Maritime History. "The Royal Navy and the Slave Trade." 2021.**
Documents the inadequacy of Squadron resources relative to mandate. Less than 5% of Royal Navy warships assigned.

**Sullivan, Anthony. Britain's War Against the Slave Trade. Pen & Sword, 2020.**
Comprehensive operational history. Heroic framing — read as factual compendium, apply critical filter.

## Pro-AI and Balanced Nonfiction — For the Debate Table

**Mollick, Ethan. Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI. Portfolio, 2024.**
Wharton professor. Empirical, cautiously optimistic, acknowledges risks. Good for a general audience skeptical of Silicon Valley cheerleading.

**Hoffman, Reid and Beato, Greg. Superagency: What Could Possibly Go Right With Our AI Future. Crown, 2024.**
Explicitly pro-AI. Argues AI augments rather than replaces human agency. Read as the serious optimist case.

**Witt, Stephen. The Thinking Machine: Jensen Huang, Nvidia, and the World's Most Coveted Microchip. Dutton, 2025.**
Hardware reality check. The chips enabling AI also enable medical imaging, climate modeling, and agricultural optimization. Excellent journalism.

**Patel, Dwarkesh et al. The Scaling Era: An Oral History of AI, 2019–2025. 2025.**
Primary source material — the people who built the technology speaking in their own voices. Captures internal debates the public narrative misses.

**Hagey, Keach. The Optimist: Sam Altman, OpenAI, and the Race to Invent the Future. Norton, 2025.**
The Economist: "It is no hagiography." Best available account of OpenAI's development. Complicated portrait of a complicated subject.

## Digital Sources and Ongoing Monitoring

The most rigorous ongoing fact-checking of AI environmental claims. Overstates the counter-case occasionally but the methodology is sound.

Max Roser's empirical case for long-term human progress. The closest available thing to a data-based pro-technology optimism resource.

**HSToday — Homeland Security Today. hstoday.us**
Professional counterterrorism and security analysis. Covering the anti-AI radicalization threat with increasing seriousness post-April 2026.

**arXiv security research — prompt injection, agentic AI vulnerabilities**
arxiv.org/search — query "prompt injection 2026", "agentic AI security". Active research area. Relevant to the technical threat from weaponized anti-AI actors.
