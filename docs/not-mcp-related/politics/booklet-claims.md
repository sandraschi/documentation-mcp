# Claims, Sources, and Corrections

*Specific factual claims circulating in anti-AI discourse · Documented sources · Documented corrections · Print and laminate for debate table use*

---

> **"A Google data center near Santiago uses more than 1,000× the water of the entire local population."**
>
> *Claim source:* Karen Hao, Empire of AI (2025), p.288
>
> **CORRECTED.** Unit conversion error: liters/second misread as cubic meters/hour. Actual figure: roughly equal to annual residential water use of the city of ~88,000 people. Off by factor of 1,000.
>
> *Correction source:* Hao correction statement, karendhao.com, Dec 2025. Independent analysis: Andy Masley, Substack, Nov 2025.

---

> **"AI will cause human extinction with 95–99% probability (p(doom))."**
>
> *Claim source:* Eliezer Yudkowsky, LessWrong, Slate, Time, various 2022–2026
>
> **UNFALSIFIABLE.** Not a scientific estimate — no methodology exists for this calculation. Unfalsifiable by construction: any outcome can be made consistent with the model. Stuart Russell (Berkeley), Geoffrey Hinton, and other credentialed AI safety researchers explicitly distance themselves from this probability estimate.
>
> *Correction source:* Russell, Human Compatible (2019). Yudkowsky's own LessWrong posts show no falsifiable prediction track record.

---

> **"Herbert's Dune provides a model for real-world anti-AI resistance."**
>
> *Claim source:* Moreno-Gama Instagram "butlerian_jihadist"; widespread in anti-AI online communities
>
> **MISREADING.** Herbert explicitly stated Dune is a warning against messianic movements, not a manual. The Butlerian Jihad is presented as a catastrophe humanity barely survived. The post-Jihad world functions only because Herbert invented magical replacements for machines (Mentats, Bene Gesserit, Guild Navigators, spice) that do not exist in reality.
>
> *Correction source:* Herbert interviews, collected in The Maker of Dune (1987). Internal textual evidence throughout the series.

---

> **"AI will eliminate most jobs within 1–2 years."**
>
> *Claim source:* Various — Microsoft AI Chief, Feb 2026; multiple tech executives 2024–2026
>
> **EXAGGERATED.** Historical pattern: automation displaces specific job categories while creating others. Displacement is real and uneven; total elimination timeline claims are consistently wrong across decades of similar predictions. Q1 2026: 27,000 disclosed AI-linked job cuts — significant, not civilizational.
>
> *Correction source:* Challenger, Gray & Christmas job cut data Q1 2026. Acemoglu & Restrepo, "Robots and Jobs" (2018) for historical baseline.

---

> **"AI data centers will consume 1.7 trillion gallons of drinkable water by 2027."**
>
> *Claim source:* Various media, citing Goldman Sachs / Morgan Stanley projections 2023–2024
>
> **MISREAD.** The original study cited specifies that only ~3% of projected water use would be potable/drinkable water, and ~90% would be returned to source unaffected. The drinkable water figure requires multiple compounding misreadings of the source.
>
> *Correction source:* Masley, Andy, "The AI Water Issue Is Fake," Substack (2025). Original Goldman Sachs report (2023).

---

> **"PauseAI and similar groups endorse the actions of Moreno-Gama."**
>
> *Claim source:* Implicit in some media framing post-April 2026
>
> **FALSE.** PauseAI explicitly condemned the attack, banned Moreno-Gama from their Discord, and stated: "Violence against anyone is antithetical to everything we stand for." Moreno-Gama was never an official member.
>
> *Correction source:* PauseAI official statement, April 10–11 2026.
