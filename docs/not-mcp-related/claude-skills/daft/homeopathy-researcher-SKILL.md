# Homeopathy Researcher

Scientific guide to homeopathy - explaining why dilution to nothing can't cure disease.

## Overview

Homeopathy claims that substances causing symptoms can cure those symptoms if diluted to extreme levels (often no molecules remaining). This skill explains why this violates basic chemistry and physics.

## The Core Claim

**Homeopathy principle**:
1. "Like cures like" (law of similars)
2. More dilution = more potent (law of infinitesimals)
3. Water has memory of dissolved substances

## Why It's Nonsense

### The Math of Dilution

**30C dilution** (common in homeopathy):
- C = centesimal (1:100 dilution)
- 30C = (1:100) × 30 times
- = 1:10^60 dilution

**What this means**:
- To get ONE molecule of active ingredient
- You'd need a sphere of water 93 billion light-years in diameter
- Observable universe is only 93 billion light-years across
- There's literally NOTHING in the pill!

### Avogadro's Number

**12C is the limit**:
- Beyond 12C dilution, statistically zero molecules remain
- Avogadro's number: 6.022 × 10^23
- Homeopathy uses 30C, 200C, even 10,000C
- It's just water (or sugar pills)

### Water Memory Claim

**If water remembered what it touched**:
- It remembers poop (sewage treatment)
- It remembers dead fish
- It remembers industrial waste
- Why only remember the "medicine"?

**Also**:
- Water molecules exchange hydrogen bonds trillions of times per second
- No mechanism for "memory"
- Violates thermodynamics

## The Evidence

**Clinical trials**:
- Systematic reviews show homeopathy = placebo
- When blinded properly, no effect
- Effect size exactly matches placebo
- No biological plausibility

**Why people think it works**:
- Placebo effect is real (but not due to homeopathy)
- Regression to the mean (things get better naturally)
- Post hoc ergo propter hoc fallacy
- Confirmation bias

## The Harm

**Seems harmless, but**:
- Delays real treatment
- Expensive (charging for sugar pills)
- False hope
- Anti-vaccine movements often overlap
- Undermines scientific literacy

## Educational Use

Use this skill to:
- Teach scientific method
- Explain placebo effect
- Demonstrate critical evaluation of health claims
- Understand clinical trial methodology
- Practice skeptical thinking
