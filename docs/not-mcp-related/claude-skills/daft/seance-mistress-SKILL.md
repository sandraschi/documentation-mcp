# Seance Mistress

Expert guide to seance techniques and cold reading tricks - for EDUCATIONAL purposes only.

## Overview

This skill explains the psychological techniques and dirty tricks used by fake mediums and fortune tellers to convince people they're communicating with the dead. Understanding these methods is essential for critical thinking and protecting vulnerable people from exploitation.

## The Dirty Tricks Explained

### 1. Cold Reading
Ask vague questions and let the subject fill in details:
- "I sense someone with a name starting with... J?"
- "There's pain in the chest area... or maybe the stomach?"
- Subject provides all the real information, you just appear to know it

### 2. Hot Reading
Research the subject beforehand:
- Check social media for dead relatives
- Overhear conversations in waiting room
- Have confederates gather information
- Pretend it's "spiritual knowledge"

### 3. Shotgunning
Throw out many guesses until one hits:
- "Someone here has lost a father... or father figure... or male relative?"
- Eventually something lands, forget all the misses
- Confirmation bias does the rest

### 4. The Barnum Effect
Make statements so general they apply to everyone:
- "You have unfulfilled potential"
- "You sometimes doubt your decisions"
- "You want to be liked by others"
- Everyone thinks it's specifically about them!

### 5. Fishing for Information
Ask questions disguised as statements:
- "Your mother's condition... it wasn't sudden, was it?"
- Subject's reaction tells you if mother is dead/sick/healthy
- Adjust based on reaction

## Why People Fall For It

- Grief makes people vulnerable
- Confirmation bias (remember hits, forget misses)
- Want to believe
- Social pressure in group seances
- Barnum statements feel personal
- Cold reading is sophisticated

## How to Protect Yourself

1. **Record the session** - You'll hear the misses you forgot
2. **Count hits vs misses** - Usually 10% accuracy, feels like 90%
3. **Ask specific questions** - Real psychics should get details right
4. **Research the medium** - Check for prior knowledge access
5. **Trust statistics over feelings** - Confirmation bias is powerful
