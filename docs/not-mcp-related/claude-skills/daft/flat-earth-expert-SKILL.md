# Flat Earth Expert

Master guide to flat Earth claims and why they're nonsense - for debunking and critical thinking education.

## Overview

This skill explains flat Earth arguments and methodically debunks them using basic physics, observable evidence, and logical reasoning. Educational tool for teaching critical thinking and scientific literacy.

## Common Claims (and Why They're Wrong)

### Claim: "The horizon looks flat"
**Rebuttal**:
- Earth is 12,742 km diameter, you're 1.7m tall
- Curvature: ~8 inches per mile squared
- At sea level, horizon is 5 km away
- Not enough distance to see obvious curve
- BUT: Watch ships disappear hull-first
- BUT: Higher altitude shows curvature (airplanes!)

### Claim: "Water finds its level"
**Rebuttal**:
- Water finds perpendicular to gravity vector
- Gravity points to Earth's center
- On a sphere, "level" is curved
- Oceans DO curve (measurable with lasers)
- Ships disappearing proves this

### Claim: "If Earth spun, we'd fly off"
**Rebuttal**:
- Centrifugal force = mv²/r
- Earth's rotation: 1,670 km/h at equator
- But radius is 6,371 km
- Centrifugal force: 0.034 m/s²
- Gravity: 9.8 m/s² (289× stronger!)
- You'd need Earth to spin 17× faster to fly off

### Claim: "Planes would have to constantly nose down"
**Rebuttal**:
- Planes follow gravity vector
- "Straight and level" means perpendicular to gravity
- Gravity points to Earth's center
- Planes naturally follow the curve
- Autopilot maintains altitude above surface (curved)

### Claim: "No one has been to Antarctica to check the ice wall"
**Rebuttal**:
- Thousands of scientists work in Antarctica
- Multiple countries have bases there
- Tourists can visit (expensive but possible)
- No ice wall found, just normal South Pole
- Circumnavigation proves it's a continent, not a wall

## Red Flags in Conspiracy Theories

1. **Unfalsifiable claims** - "Government hides evidence"
2. **Cherry-picking data** - Ignore 99% of evidence
3. **Misunderstanding science** - Don't grasp basic physics
4. **Ad hominem attacks** - "Scientists are lying"
5. **Moving goalposts** - When proven wrong, change claim
6. **Special pleading** - "Rules of physics don't apply here"

## Why People Believe

- Distrust of authorities (sometimes justified!)
- Community and belonging
- Feeling "special" with secret knowledge
- Dunning-Kruger effect (don't realize what they don't know)
- YouTube algorithm amplifies fringe content
- Confirmation bias
