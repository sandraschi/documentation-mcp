## Deal: investment, not a grant

- Standard deal for every accepted company: **$500k total** = $125k on a post-money SAFE for a fixed **7%**, plus $375k on an **uncapped MFN SAFE** (converts at your best later terms; e.g. 2.5% at a $15M cap).
- Wired starting at **acceptance**, not batch start. No milestones, no fees, no gotcha downside terms. YC keeps **pro-rata rights** into later rounds.
- Older batches had smaller checks (hence stray "$250k"-type figures in the wild). Quote the live page, not memory.

## Process: garage to batch

- Apply online (+ short video) to one of four yearly batches; video interview, usually same-day decision with feedback. Reapplying with progress is normal.
- Batch is **in person in San Francisco** (3-day kickoff, weekly small-group dinners, dedicated ex-founder partner, speaker sessions, $12M+ in cloud/API credits), ending in investor intros. Alumni network persists for life - that is half the product.
- They fund idea-stage teams, but team beats idea: 2-3 full-time cofounders, roughly equal split, at least one deeply technical. Five cofounders, part-timers, or non-builders are negative signals. Show build velocity, not slides.

## International founders (incl. Vienna)

- Apply from anywhere. Must be a US, Canada, Cayman, or Singapore corporation - otherwise you **flip** (local entity becomes subsidiary of a new parent holding the IP; YC introduces lawyers). Batch still requires ~3 months of SF presence. So an Vienna garage is eligible, but budget the flip plus the California quarter.

## Precedents (both doors)

Flip-and-fly (did YC from abroad): Stripe (Ireland, S09), GitLab (Ukraine/Netherlands, W15), Algolia (France, W14), GoCardless (UK, S11), Onfido (Oxford founders), Strikingly (China, W13), Rappi (Colombia, W16), Paystack (Nigeria, W16). Institutional episode: YC China ran 2018-2019 under Qi Lu, then wound down and routed Chinese founders into US batches.

Skipped-YC-and-won (no batch, no permission): Peter Steinberger (Vienna/Linz - PSPDFKit/Nutrient, then OpenClaw virality), Bitpanda and GoStudent (Vienna unicorns via classic European VC). The Vienna garage is missing no required ingredient either way.
