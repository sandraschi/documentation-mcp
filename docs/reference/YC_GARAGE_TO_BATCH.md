# Vienna garage to YC batch - founder onboarding plan

**Status:** Reference v1 2026-10-05 - the detailed path behind [YC_STANDARD_DEAL.md](./YC_STANDARD_DEAL.md). Terms verified 2026-10-05; costs are ranges, re-verify before budgeting; legal/visa rows are pointers, not advice - talk to a lawyer, not to an AI.
**Tags:** [fleet, reference, y-combinator, fundraising, onboarding]
**Audience:** A technical team sitting (say) in Vienna with an idea, asking what the road to a batch actually looks like.

## The two doors

Door A is YC: $500k standard deal, 3-month SF batch, investor intros, alumni for life. Door B is independent: European VC or bootstrapping to virality (Bitpanda, GoStudent, Steinberger - all Vienna, none did the batch). This plan walks Door A. Keep Door B in mind at every phase; several phases (team, build) are identical for both.

## Phase 0 - team (weeks 1-2 of honesty)

- Target: 2-3 cofounders, full-time, roughly equal split, at least one deeply technical. YC funds teams, not ideas.
- Five friends in a garage is a starting crowd, not a founding team. Cut to the core who will go full-time; convert the rest to early employees, advisors with 0.1-0.5%, or friends. Painful now beats fatal at the interview (YC partners ask "who is CEO and who can be fired" in so many words).
- Everyone full-time or everyone out. No part-timers, no thesis-on-the-side. This is also the single most common interview rejection reason.

## Phase 1 - build (4-8 weeks, before applying)

- Demo beats deck. Ship the ugliest version that a stranger can touch, then talk to users weekly and let the demo change. YC application reviewers score team velocity + market + traction; the idea ranks last.
- For robotics/hardware (the Nori shape): video of the thing moving in a real room beats renders. Fleet tie-in: if your robot has an SDK, a `*-mcp` wrapper is a shockingly good demo artifact - it shows developers can already build on you.
- Do not incorporate yet if you have not (YC helps post-acceptance). Do start the IP hygiene: all code/work assigned to the future company in writing, even among friends.

## Phase 2 - incorporate / flip (parallel with Phase 3)

- YC invests only in US, Canada, Cayman, or Singapore parents. Austrian team therefore flips: typically a new Delaware C-corp becomes the parent, the Austrian entity (GmbH, or since 2024 the lighter FlexKap) becomes its subsidiary and keeps operating locally. IP ultimately sits under the parent.
- Cost shape: Stripe Atlas–style incorporation around $500; a real flip with lawyers more like low five figures - get a quote, do not budget from this page. YC introduces lawyers post-acceptance, so many teams apply unflipped and flip on acceptance.
- Bank account, cap table (equal-ish split, 10%+ option pool planned), founder vesting with a one-year cliff. Vesting among friends feels insulting until the first departure; do it anyway.

## Phase 3 - apply (one evening + one video)

- Online form plus a 1-minute video, four batches a year (Winter apps close early Nov, batch Jan-Mar; Spring/Summer/Fall cycle similarly - check the live /apply page).
- The form is short on purpose: what are you building, who are you, what have you shipped, how do you make money. Answer in specifics with dates and numbers, not adjectives. Reviewers read thousands; "grew from 3 to 40 pilot users Sept-Oct" beats "revolutionary platform."
- Apply when Phase 1 shows motion, not when it feels ready. Reapplying with progress is normal and respected.

## Phase 4 - interview (10 minutes)

- Video call, rapid fire, same-day decision with written feedback either way. They probe founder-market fit ("why you"), growth ("what happens if this works"), and honesty ("what is broken right now").
- Bring the demo, the numbers, and the cofounders. No slides needed. The correct answer to an unknown is "we do not know yet, here is how we would find out."

## Phase 5 - the California quarter (logistics)

- Batch is in person in SF: 3-day kickoff plus regular meetups across ~3 months. Budget short-term housing per person in the low thousands per month (mission/SOMA rooms, verify current listings), plus food, flights, health cover. Rule of thumb: the quarter costs a small car per founder.
- Visa (Austrians): the 3-month batch fits inside ESTA's 90 days, but ESTA is not work authorization - YC's lawyers advise founders on the correct status (commonly B-1 business visitor; O-1 later for some). Get this advice from them, not from this page.
- Runway math: $500k at, say, $25k/month burn for a team of three living cheaply is ~18 months to reach seed metrics. Write the burn plan before landing.

## Phase 6 - batch survival

- Weekly partner meeting (numbers up or explanation ready), weekly dinners with your small group (lifelong network - take it seriously), one metric that matters moved every week, launch something small early in the batch for the momentum.
- Take the $12M+ in partner credits (cloud, APIs, Stripe) on day one; it is real money.
- Talk to users every week, including weeks you want to hide and code. Especially those weeks.

## Phase 7 - Demo Day to seed

- YC introduces its investor network at the end of the batch. The $375k MFN SAFE from the standard deal converts on your best seed terms, so a clean seed (say $2-4M on a post-money SAFE) sets everyone's economics at once.
- YC exercises pro-rata in many later rounds - expect them on your cap table for years, which is a feature (signal + help) until it is not (follow-on dynamics). Price it in.
- If the seed does not come together: default alive (cut burn, extend runway) beats default dead. The alumni network helps either way - that part was not marketing.

## Vienna notes

- Local entity choice (GmbH vs FlexKap) matters only for the subsidiary; the parent is what YC prices. Decide with the lawyer, not over coffee.
- Time zones: batch runs on SF time; warn families, partners, and cats.
- The independent door stays open the whole way: if batch economics (flip cost + quarter cost + 7% + MFN slice) exceed the value of the network for a hardware-heavy team with Viennese VC access, walking away pre-acceptance costs nothing. Steipete is the local proof.
