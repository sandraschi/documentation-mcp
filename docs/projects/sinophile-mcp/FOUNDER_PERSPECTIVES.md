## 1. Characters vs global leadership

**Opinion:** The writing system is an **albatross** — a permanent drag on China playing the role of undisputed **"leader of the planet"** in a modern, information-speed world. Literacy and elite gatekeeping cost more than in alphabetized societies; software and science publishing still bend toward Latin script.

**Opinion:** The practical **saviour** is widespread adoption of **English** as the global working language (science, aviation, internet stack, finance). China **does not like** that dependency — and actively promotes Mandarin abroad — but English still wins on network effects. Sinophile should **teach Chinese seriously** while acknowledging this tension honestly in the Language → Writing tab (see [SCRIPT_REFORM_BRIEF.md](./SCRIPT_REFORM_BRIEF.md)), not as anti-China snark.

**For Knowledge writers:** Document PRC language policy, Confucius Institutes, and domestic nationalism **with dates**; do not adopt this section as wiki voice.

## 2. The Ming inward turn (~500 years ago)

**Opinion:** An **idiotic isolationist inward turn** after the treasure-fleet age was a civilizational fork — among history's worst **footguns**. Zheng He was **lucky not to be executed**; the **fleet was burned / dismantled** and ocean voyages killed by policy, not by accident. China had looked ready to **revolutionize trade and cultural exchange** and pull ahead of Europe; instead the Ming elite chose inward tribute theater and maritime self-amputation. See [MING_MARITIME_FOOTGUN.md](./MING_MARITIME_FOOTGUN.md).

**For Knowledge writers:** Document the timeline **hard** (1405–1433 voyages → halt → fleet destruction/bans → European oceanic century). **Do not** lead with "historians argue" hedging — that reads like **nationalist revisionist whitewash** of the Ming turn. Detail disputes (smuggling, silver, regional trade) come **after** the main fact of state abandonment of the program, not as an excuse. Charter-neutral still means **no PRC apology prose** and **no sinophobe glee** — just stop softening the footgun.
