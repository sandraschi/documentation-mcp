# sinophile-mcp — editorial charter (MCD draft)

**Status:** DRAFT · **Applies when:** `sinophile-mcp` repo is scaffolded  
**Parent plan:** [PHILE_SISTERS_PLAN.md](../PHILE_SISTERS_PLAN.md)  
**Owner stance:** Deep love of Chinese language and civilization (**sinophile** in the plain sense) — not an apology for any state, party, or foreign policy line.

---

## 1. Two failure modes (both forbidden in Knowledge)

| Failure mode | What it looks like | Why we reject it |
|--------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **PRC propaganda** | Timeless civilization, inevitable reunification, no mistakes since 1949, Taiwan "always was" a province | Erases dates, indigenous history, and documented policy shifts; CCP is **no slouch at lying with the best of them** — treat official narratives as **primary sources to cite and compare**, not as article voice |
| **Sinophobe cottage industry** | Especially US: racialized "China bad" memes, Cold War 2 cheerleading, Taiwan as **suicide proxy war** fantasy, conflation of party/state/people | Racist hatred dressed as analysis; feeds the "war with China over Taiwan" idiocy without teaching history |

**Goal:** Learner-trustworthy pages — dated facts, named actors, footnotes / further reading. The skill (`sinophile-expert`) **declines** to produce either flavor of agitprop.

---

## 2. Voice rules for Knowledge HTML

1. **Lead with chronology and geography**, not moral verdicts in the first paragraph.  
2. **Separate layers:** ethnicity / language / state / party / empire / colony — do not swap them casually.  
3. **Name what is disputed** (PRC vs ROC legal claims, scholar X vs Y) **after** established facts are on the page — never open the Ming maritime reversal or Taiwan with **"historians argue"** fog ([LOGIC_AND_FALLACIES.md](./LOGIC_AND_FALLACIES.md) · Pirie, *Book of the Fallacy*, Calibre).  
4. **No genocide olympics** — document atrocities with sources (Great Leap, Cultural Revolution, Feb 28, White Terror, etc.) without competitive suffering rhetoric.  
5. **Language learning stays upbeat; history stays careful** — Language tabs can be enthusiastic; Knowledge tabs read like a good university survey, not a thread.  
6. **Chat skill:** may explain learner questions with the same rules; must not simulate MFA press releases or congressional hawk talking points.

---

## 3. Flagship hard content: Taiwan (required before "modern politics" cluster ships)

Taiwan is the **bugbear cluster**: everyone wants you to pick a team. We ship **one long reference article** (or split series) following [TAIWAN_KNOWLEDGE_OUTLINE.md](./sinophile-mcp/TAIWAN_KNOWLEDGE_OUTLINE.md).

Minimum bar before publishing:

- Indigenous Austronesian peoples (pre-Han majority societies)  
- Han migration, Qing administration, marginality, piracy / "backwater" centuries  
- 1895–1945 Japanese colonial period (modernization + assimilation pressure)  
- 1945 handover, Feb 28, White Terror, KMT one-party era (violent rule **then** democratization **later** — same party, different eras)  
- Economic takeoff, supply chains, **TSMC** and semiconductor story (technical + geopolitical, sourced)  
- Cross-strait economic entanglement (investment, talent, supply chains) — facts both sides downplay  
- PRC legal/political claims vs ROC institutions vs **Taiwanese identity** surveys (cite years)  
- US role: Taiwan Relations Act, strategic ambiguity, arms sales — **without** treating war as inevitable or desirable  
- DPP / KMT / other parties as **domestic Taiwanese politics**, not cartoon villains; reject foreign "separatist warmonger" and "reunification or bust" slogans equally when they erase nuance  

Explicit **anti-goal:** Do not write a page that sounds like preparation for kinetic war. Call the **"war with China over Taiwan"** discourse what it is: mostly foreign policy theatre that ignores island history.

---

## 4. Review process (sinophile-only)

1. First draft: human author + citations list (no LLM-only publish on Taiwan/PRC/xinjiang/tibet-class topics).  
2. Second pass: "propaganda sniff" checklist (§1) + date sanity.  
3. Optional: external reader (Taiwan-based or diaspora) before public Glama listing.  
4. Version footer on HTML: `Last reviewed YYYY-MM`.
