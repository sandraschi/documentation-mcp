## "Historians argue …" in our work

Opening Ming/Taiwan/modern PRC history with **"historians argue"** / **"debated narrative"** fog — before dated facts — is not neutral scholarship in learner-facing HTML. It is **false balance**: a documented policy turn (treasure fleet halted, ships destroyed, ocean bans) treated as optional opinion.

**Shelf it in Pirie terms:** treat that opener like a **fallacy move** (confusion of dispute over details with dispute over whether the event happened; **appeal to controversy** dressed as sophistication). Fine in polemic; **forbidden as lead** in sinophile Knowledge — see [MING_MARITIME_FOOTGUN.md](./MING_MARITIME_FOOTGUN.md), [EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md](./EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md) rule 2.3.

**Same racket, different topic:** **Appeal to controversy** is stock in trade for **Holocaust deniers** ("historians still debate"), **quack medicine** pushers ("experts disagree"), climate/obfuscation merchants, and other unsavory types who want **equal billing for fringe and fact** before evidence is on the table. Ming maritime **bothsidesism** uses the same shape — not morally equivalent subject matter, **equivalent bad faith move**. Our Knowledge pages do not lend that move respectability.
