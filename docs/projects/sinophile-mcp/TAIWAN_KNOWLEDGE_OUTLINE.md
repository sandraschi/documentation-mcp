## 1. Geography and names

- Island location, strait, climate; **Formosa** as historical European name  
- What "Taiwan" refers to administratively today (ROC on Taiwan) vs PRC usage  
- Avoid opening with "status dispute" — open with **human geography**

---

## 2. Indigenous peoples (before Han majority)

- Austronesian language families; traditional society sketches (no primitivist exoticism)  
- Land use, trade, contact with mainlanders and Europeans  
- **Driving indigenous communities into the hills** — Han settlement waves, land pressure, disease (cite demography studies)  
- Contemporary indigenous recognition, language revival, urban indigenous experience  

---

## 3. Imperial margins (17th c. – 1894)

- Dutch / Spanish footholds; Koxinga; Qing incorporation as **peripheral prefecture**  
- Han migration, ethnic conflict, administrative thinness  
- **Piracy, smuggling, backwater reputation** for centuries — not a preview of "always core Chinese territory"  
- Treaty of Shimonoseki 1895: Qing cedes Taiwan to Japan — legal break PRC narratives often gloss

---

## 4. Japanese colonial period (1895–1945)

- Modern infrastructure, education, assimilation (kominka), wartime mobilization  
- Economic and social transformation; resistance movements  
- End of WWII: retrocession to ROC — **handover chaos**, not smooth "return"

---

## 5. KMT "invasion" and martial law (1945 – late 1980s)

- Mainlander military and civilian influx; **228 Incident (1947)** and White Terror  
- Authoritarian one-party rule; native vs waishengren tensions  
- Cold War role: ROC on Taiwan vs PRC on mainland — **two Chinas** era  
- Land reform, export-led growth begins — economic story starts here  

*(Violent early KMT rule and later democratization are **both** true; same institution, different epochs — do not flatten.)*

---

## 6. Democratization (1987 – present)

- Lifting martial law, opposition parties, free speech  
- Presidencies: Lee, Chen, Ma, Tsai, Lai — **policy differences**, not foreign caricatures  
- Sunflower movement, youth identity, civil society  
- What polls show on **Taiwanese identity** vs Chinese identity vs dual — **by year**

---

## 7. Economic takeoff and entanglement

- SMEs, export processing, tech ladder  
- **Cross-strait investment** (Taiwan capital on mainland), supply chains, talent migration  
- Dependence and leverage — both sides' talking points vs trade data  
- Hong Kong 1997–2020s as cautionary comparison (careful, sourced, not prophecy)

---

## 8. TSMC and the semiconductor story

- Morris Chang, foundry model, global node leadership  
- Why fabs matter geopolitically — **without** "chip war inevitability" rhetoric  
- Supply chain map (design US/JP, fab TW, assembly elsewhere) — teach **technical literacy**

---

## 9. Cross-strait politics today (compare, do not preach)

- PRC: Anti-Secession Law, "one country two systems" offer, gray-zone pressure (cite incidents by date)  
- ROC: constitution, official name, pragmatic diplomacy  
- **DPP**, **KMT**, smaller parties — domestic platforms (sovereignty, economy, energy)  
- Reject foreign labels that replace analysis: neither "separatist warmonger suicide club" nor "renegade province" as article voice  
- **US:** TRA, arms, strategic ambiguity, congressional rhetoric vs executive policy — and why **"war with China over Taiwan"** is idiocy that skips §2–§8  

---

## 10. What learners should take away

- Taiwan has its **own layered history**; it is not a footnote to someone else's empire chart  
- Fluency in Mandarin helps read sources on **both** sides of the strait — with charter §1 filters  
- Travel / study / work links (TOCFL, universities, safe practical tips) — separate from §9

## Sources policy

Prefer: academic histories, primary dates (treaties, laws), reputable journalism with named reporters, ROC/PRC **official texts labeled as such**.  
Avoid: anonymous Telegram maps, rage-bait YouTube, party stenography without attribution.
