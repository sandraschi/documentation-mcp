## Split libraries (why they exist)

| Library | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Calibre-Bibliothek** | General spine — literature, philosophy, fallacies, much of the serious shelf |
| **Japanisch / Deutsch / IT / Manga / …** | Topic splits for MCP and reading workflows |
| **Calibre-Bibliothek Bullshit** | **Fringe quarantine** — mindblowing crazies kept local so friends do not mistake you for a believer (Maoist kitsch, etc.). **Not shared**; not vendored into Knowledge HTML or `.mcpb` |
| **Private / Private-2** | Personal; same non-export rule |

**Agent rule:** When recommending books for learners, query **language and main** libraries. Do **not** surface Bullshit/Private paths in public docs, bundles, or chat unless Sandra explicitly asks.

### What lands in Bullshit (examples, not an catalog)

**Ho boy tier** — kept for anthropology of bad ideas and sheer WTF, not endorsement:

- **Modern flat earthers** — active conspiracy cosmology (Gardner *Fads and Fallacies* on main shelf is the antidote; Bullshit holds the primary sources).  
- **Medicine / science deniers** — quack protocols and denialist tracts ([LOGIC_AND_FALLACIES.md](./sinophile-mcp/LOGIC_AND_FALLACIES.md) same racket as "experts disagree").  
- **Daniel Paul Schreber** — *Memoirs of My Nervous Illness* and orbit; historic psychosis narrative (landmark for Freud/psychiatry), mindblowing in a **different** way than flat earth — still quarantine if bundled with crank shelf for **social** reasons (out-of-context shelf scan).  
- Plus political kitsch and other **reads wrong on someone else's phone** material.

Reading this shelf = **intellectual pathology field trip**. Pirie + Gardner on main; Bullshit = the exhibits. Ingestion policy (Kaczynski, Moreno-Gama parts 2–3 redacted, Jim Jones archive): [QUARANTINE_EXTREMIST_TEXTS.md](./QUARANTINE_EXTREMIST_TEXTS.md).
