# Jack Foley: The Pioneer of Sound Design

**Role**: Universal Studios Sound Specialist (1891–1967)  
**Legacy**: Invented the art of "Foley" sound effects.

## 🥥 The Father of "Clip-Clop"
**Jack Foley** is one of the most influential figures in cinema history, yet his name has been genericized into a technical term—a fate shared by few, such as *Hoover* or *Xerox*. 

In the late 1920s, as the film industry transitioned from silent films to "talkies," Jack Foley revolutionized how movies sounded. While filming the 1927 silent film *Show Boat*, Universal decided to turn it into a musical. Since they couldn't reshoot the entire film with sound, Jack Foley and his team projected the film on a screen and performed the sound effects live on a single track.

### The Original Techniques
Jack Foley was a master of using mundane objects to create cinematic illusions:
- **Coconuts**: The classic method for horse hooves.
- **Leather Gloves**: Flapping them together to simulate a bird’s wings.
- **Cellophane**: Crinkling it to create the sound of a crackling fire.
- **Corn Starch**: Stepping on bags of it to simulate walking on snow.

## 🎭 The Foley Stage
Because of his work, the space where sound effects are recorded today is called a **Foley Stage**, and the professionals who perform these sounds are called **Foley Artists**. 

Jack Foley’s genius was in **timing**. He understood that sound is not just an acoustic event, but an emotive one. A footstep isn't just noise; it conveys the character's weight, mood, and environment.

## 🚀 From Physical to Neural
In the Fleet’s documentation, we distinguish between:
1. **Classical Foley**: Physical performance on a stage (Jack’s legacy).
2. **Digital Foley**: Using sample libraries (The 1990s–2020s standard).
3. **Neural Foley**: Generative models like **[FoleyCrafter](foleycrafter.md)** that "watch" video and synthesize sound physics automatically (The 2026 SOTA).
