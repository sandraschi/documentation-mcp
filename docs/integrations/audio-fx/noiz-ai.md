# Noiz AI: Cinematic Foley Control

**Category**: Audio FX / Foley Design  
**Status**: SOTA Physics Model (v1.4.0)  
**Primary Strength**: Material Physics & Envelope Control

## Overview
**Noiz AI** is a specialized generator built for sound designers. Unlike generic text-to-audio models, Noiz layers a physics-based engine over its neural network, allowing for precise control over how a sound "behaves" in a virtual space.

## 🛠️ Advanced Controls
Noiz AI provides unique parameters that are essential for high-end Foley work:

### 1. Material Physics
Specify the density and texture of the sound source:
- `Wood`: Hollow, resonant, warm.
- `Metal`: Sharp transients, harmonic ringing.
- `Stone`: Gritty, heavy impacts.
- `Fabric`: Soft, high-frequency "fizz."

### 2. Envelope Design (ADSR)
- **Attack**: How fast the sound hits (e.g., a "sharp" punch vs. a "soft" push).
- **Decay/Sustain**: The length of the initial sound body.
- **Release**: The natural fade-out or reverb tail.

## 🎬 Example Workflow: The "Coconuts"
To recreate the Jack Foley coconut hoist:
1. **Prompt**: `Rhythmic hoofbeats on cobblestone path.`
2. **Material**: `Hardwood` (for the coconut shell texture).
3. **Environment**: `Stone Alley` (for the reverb profile).
4. **Attack**: `Ultra-Fast` (for the clean "clack" sound).

---
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Noiz AI is best used when you need to "match" a sound exactly to an action, as its parameters allow you to fine-tune the timing and texture to sub-millisecond precision.
