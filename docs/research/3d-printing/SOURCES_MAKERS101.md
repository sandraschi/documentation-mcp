# Source Note: makers101.com (2026-10-18)

**What:** Independent 3D-printing / laser / scanning review site by Nik (Shenzhen, Guangdong). Printing since 2022, writing since end of 2025. 90+ articles, ~10k readers/mo. Also `youtube.com/@makers101com`, `facebook.com/makers101diy`.

**Why it is useful for mcd:** the only source we found that checks Creality crowdfunding math instead of reprinting it. Used for both SparkX i8 and M1/R1 notes.

* Real-use testing, not unboxings: 2-4 weeks regular use before publishing, real jobs not just cal cubes. Standard battery: full-bed cold first layer, overhangs 45/60/70, 8h+ long-print stability, noise at rated speed, adhesion/release across PLA/PETG/TPU, jam/pause recovery, plus support experience + firmware update + parts availability.
* Combines hands-on with months of Reddit / community owner reports for failure patterns one unit cannot show (e.g. i7 nozzle clog threads, M1 diameter threads).
* Shenzhen proximity: Creality / Anycubic hardware an hour away, sees machines before non-CN reviewers.
* Publishes method: `/review-methodology/` (timeline, Recommended vs Not Recommended) + `/editorial-policy/` (selection, commercial ties, corrections). Admits misses on record (heater-block failure at ~200h after an enthusiastic review, underestimating home-office noise).
* Money: Amazon Associates affiliate links, disclosed, claims no influence on verdicts. Worth knowing when prices are the story.

**Caveats (use it right):**

* One person, one unit per review, publisher history measured in months, not years. Laser coverage thinner (started 2025, says so himself).
* Creality-supplied pre-production units on beta firmware for i8 (JJ Shankles + TheLayeredWorkshop via Creality) and M1/R1 (BuildDanielBuild prototype). Directionally useful, not production evidence. He flags this; we repeat the flag in our notes.
* No lab gear: diameter checks are caliper math (e.g. `(d/1.75)^2` volume), not metrology. Treat his ROI calculator as a worksheet with your own scrap weights, not a verdict.
* Cross-check crowdfunding status against primary sources: Indiegogo comments/updates, `forum.creality.com`, `crowdfunding@creality.com` replies, `r/Creality`.
