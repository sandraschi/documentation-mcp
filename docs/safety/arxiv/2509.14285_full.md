Title: A Multi-Agent LLM Defense Pipeline Against Prompt Injection Attacks
Authors: S M Asif Hossain, Ruksat Khan Shayoni, Mohd Ruhul Ameen, Akif Islam, M. F. Mridha, Jungpil Shin
Conference: IEEE WIECON-ECE 2025

Structure:
I. Introduction
II. Related Work (Attack Taxonomy, Existing Defenses, Multi-Agent Frameworks)
III. Methodology (Attack Dataset Construction, Multi-Agent Pipeline Architectures - Chain-of-Agents, Coordinator)
IV. Experimental Setup
V. Results (Attack Success Rate, Category-Specific Analysis)
VI. Defense Architecture Effectiveness
VII. Conclusion

Key Results: 100% mitigation (ASR reduced from 20-30% to 0%) across 55 unique attacks, 400 instances.
