## 🛠️ Switching Protocols (IDE Hopping)

When facing "High Usage" or recursive timeouts:
1.  **Do Not Retry (Recursive Fail)**: If a tool call fails with a platform-tier error, do NOT attempt more than TWO retries. It wastes credits and context.
2.  **State Sync**: Commit all changes or update the `task.md` before switching IDEs to ensure no state loss.
3.  **Handoff**: Use `adn_content("quick", ...)` or a localized `VIBE_OPERATIONS.md` update to inform the next agent where we left off.
